## Buffer/step/terminal ownership
Activebuffers keyed byassistantMessageId, boundtosession/owner. Append rawdeltas in order; flushallpendingbytes when cumulativeUTF8bytes>=2048 or2000ms afterthefirstpendingdelta, whicheverfirst. Laterdeltas do not postpone thetimer. Successfulflush resetsbytes/deadline; no timerwhenpendingempty. Flush isappend-once to durablecontent; do not accumulate/copytheentireever-growing answer for eachdelta. No sleep-based timing assertions.
Steps persistimmediately; startStep requires an activeassistant, enforces SQLordinaluniqueness, andtimestampsnow; finishStep updatesonlyarunningstep, preservesdetail unlessnewdetail supplied, timestampsend. Inactive/stale append/finishStep/finishTurn returnfalse withoutmutation; invalidstartStep rejectsnot_found. Session/messageidentityguards prevent latework fromrewritingterminal rows or a laterturn.
finishTurn atomically drains residualbody, finalizesassistant+session withstatus andsessionupdatedAtnow, andsettles remainingrunningsteps tothesamestatus/endedAtnow whilepreservingalreadyterminalsteps. It cancels ownedtimers; aftercommit releaseadmission/buffer state. SQLfailure leavesbuffer/turnrecoverable, cancels scheduledretrywork andpropagates; no silentlylost/duplicateddelta. A timerflushfailure retainsbuffer, disablesautomaticretry, notifiesonFlushError exactlyonce; appendDelta onthatfaulted-but-active buffer SHALL throwthe retainedflushfault withoutgrowingthebuffer, distinctfromtheinactive/stale false path. Caller can explicitlyfinish/close afterrepair; no hiddenretryloop.
close cancelsalltimers, finalizes anyownedactive turns asfailed (drainingreceivedresidual), rejects newmutations aftersuccess, andisidempotent; it nevercloses caller-ownedDB. Onfailure exposeerror, keepuncommitted data/state for anexplicitretry, no scheduledwork left. This enables#102runtime→store→DB teardown without implementing it here.
